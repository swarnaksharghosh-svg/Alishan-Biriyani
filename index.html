Our Story

Where Every Grain Tells a Royal Tale

At Alishan Hotel & Restaurant, biryani is a ritual, not a recipe. We marinate overnight, layer long-grain rice with saffron and caramelised onions, seal the handi and let slow dum do the rest. The result is rice that's fluffy, meat that falls off the bone, and an aroma you'll smell from the street.

Beyond biryani, our kitchen serves sizzling kebabs, crispy Bengali starters, two dozen kinds of momo and combos priced for every pocket, all cooked fresh near Sundarban Adarsha Vidyamandir, Kakdwip.
